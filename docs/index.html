Post-training quantization · Measured on Qwen2.5-0.5B

Compressing models the way synapses store strength: in discrete quanta, with the critical few protected exactly.

CDQ-LRR tiles LLM weights into 32-element dendritic microdomains, normalizes each with an FP8 scale, keeps the top 1.56% salient weights bit-exact, and snaps the rest to a 26-state CaMKII-inspired lattice. Result: 5.85 bits/weight, 0.982 logit alignment — with every number below actually measured.

September 2026 · Single-model study (0.5B) · All claims below are measurements unless labeled projection.

Quantized weights on HF GitHub repo

The method

Phase 1 · NormalizePer-32-tile scale σ = max|w|, stored FP8-E4M3. x = w/σ.
Phase 2 · TagTop 1.56% by |w|·(tile_var+ε) → exact BF16 residual stream.
Phase 3 · SnapRemaining 98.44% → nearest of 26 quasi-log centroids (5-bit).

Decode is on-the-fly: ŵ = σ·LUT[q] against a 52-byte table pinned in registers, residuals injected by warp broadcast, straight into GEMV. Norms, embeddings and lm_head are never touched. C++ and Metal kernels ship in csrc/.

What the numbers say. Doubling residuals (5.6M→11.2M) moved logit cosine 0.9485→0.9514 — the 4-bit shortfall is bulk-resolution-limited (14 non-zero centroids over 4,864-dim dots), not outlier coverage. The 26-state lattice is the proven minimum here; 4-bit survives only as a draft tier.
Theory vs measurement. The accompanying theory doc projects 3.94 BPW assuming 3.35-bit index entropy; we measure 4.34 bits → 5.85 BPW actual. Its 70B-class accuracy tables are projections. This page reports only what ran.
Smartness, honestly. On 48 hand-written MCQs, v1 scores 23/48 vs baseline 29/48 (McNemar p≈0.055 — borderline). Factual recall is intact (science 8/10 both); symbolic reasoning drops first. Small-n, indicative, open to a 200-item rerun.

Scaling outlook — moderated

Tested: Qwen2.5-0.5B-Instruct only. Not tested: anything larger. A 9B validation (MiMo-V2.6-Distill-Qwen-9B, 18.8 GB) is downloading — shard 1 of 4 has taken over a day on a very slow connection in Egypt.

Reasons for cautious optimism: per-tile weight statistics are local properties, and ~1–2% outlier fractions are reported across model scales. Reason for caution: bulk error compounds with depth — already visible at 24 layers — so 70–80-layer models may demand at least the 5-bit resolution, never less. Do not cite large-model numbers until the 9B trial lands.
